What the outreach steps do

StepWhat it doesNot
draft_founder_emailWrites a short email asking for a 30-minute call and any key numbers the screen could not find. Saves a draft.Never sends. Only for a 'take the call' applicant, or when a partner asks.
read_founder_replyClassifies the pasted reply as accept, decline or ambiguous. A hedged accept becomes ambiguous.Does not guess what a mixed reply means; ambiguous goes to a person.
