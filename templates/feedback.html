How corrections are used

What counts

A real change to the score with a reason. A move under 1.0, or no reason: not stored.

What gets reused

Same criterion, similar company (picked by meaning from the description and sector). Unrelated companies get nothing.

When two disagree

The more specific one wins (same sector); if equal, the most recent. The adjustment is capped at +/-2 and shown on the applicant page with the reason.
